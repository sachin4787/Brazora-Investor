    THE BATANA STORY

    Powered by Batana. Designed for professional care.

    Premium product education, professional benefits and a three-step salon system built around Batana oil and Plex/bond-support technology.

    WHAT IS BATANA?

    Nature-led nourishment for the hair fibre.

    Batana oil is traditionally made from the nuts or kernels of the American oil palm Elaeis oleifera. It contains fatty acids, antioxidants, carotenoids and vitamin-E-type compounds.

    A Batana Plex treatment is generally a cosmetic hair treatment containing Batana oil, sometimes combined with other conditioning ingredients. The exact formula depends on the product or salon.

    WHAT DO BATANA SEEDS DO FOR HAIR?
    • Condition and soften dry hair.
    • Add shine and help reduce the appearance of frizz.
    • Coat the hair shaft and help reduce friction and breakage during combing and styling.
    • Help hair feel smoother and more manageable.
    • Provide fatty acids and antioxidant compounds that support the hair's conditioned appearance.
